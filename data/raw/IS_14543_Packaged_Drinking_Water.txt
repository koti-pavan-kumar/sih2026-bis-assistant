IS 14543:2022
Packaged Drinking Water (Other Than Mineral Water) — Specification

Specification

This standard specifies requirements for packaged drinking water other than mineral water.

Scope: This standard applies to packaged drinking water produced by purification, treatment, or any other process and sold in sealed containers. It covers water intended for human consumption and domestic use.

Clause 4: Definition
Packaged drinking water: Water that is obtained from any source, purified by processes such as reverse osmosis, distillation, deionisation, or any other appropriate method, and packed in suitable containers.

Clause 5: Requirements
Water shall be free from disease-causing organisms, harmful substances, and shall meet the requirements specified in Table 1.

Physical Requirements:
- pH value: 6.5 to 8.5
- Total dissolved solids (TDS): Maximum 500 mg/L
- Turbidity: Maximum 1 NTU
- Colour: Maximum 5 TCU (True Colour Units)
- Taste and odour: Unobjectionable
- Free from foreign matter

Chemical Requirements:
- Fluoride: Maximum 1.0 mg/L
- Chlorides: Maximum 250 mg/L
- Sulphates: Maximum 200 mg/L
- Iron: Maximum 0.3 mg/L
- Total hardness as CaCO3: Maximum 200 mg/L
- Copper: Maximum 0.05 mg/L
- Zinc: Maximum 5.0 mg/L
- Lead: Maximum 0.01 mg/L
- Arsenic: Maximum 0.01 mg/L
- Mercury: Maximum 0.001 mg/L
- Cadmium: Maximum 0.003 mg/L
- Chromium (Hexavalent): Maximum 0.05 mg/L
- Nitrate: Maximum 45 mg/L
- Total coliform: Absent in 100 mL
- E. coli: Absent in 100 mL

Microbiological Requirements:
- Aerobic mesophilic count at 22°C: Maximum 100 CFU/mL
- Aerobic mesophilic count at 36°C: Maximum 10 CFU/mL
- Total coliform bacteria: Absent in 100 mL
- Pseudomonas aeruginosa: Absent in 25 mL

Clause 6: Packaging
Water shall be packed in clean, new, and tamper-evident containers made of food-grade polyethylene or polyethylene terephthalate (PET). The container shall be sealed to prevent contamination during storage and transport.

Clause 7: Labeling
Labels shall include:
- Product name: "Packaged Drinking Water"
- Net quantity in litres or millilitres
- Name and address of the packer/bottler
- Batch number
- Date of packaging (day, month, year)
- Best before date or expiry date
- BIS standard mark (IS 14543)
- Consumer care details
- FSSAI license number

Test Methods:
- pH measurement: IS 3025 (Part 12)
- TDS determination: IS 3025 (Part 16)
- Turbidity: IS 3025 (Part 18)
- Microbiological testing: IS 5402
- Chemical analysis: IS 3025 (Part 18 to Part 52)
