IS 15556:2023
LED Lamps for General Lighting — Performance Requirements

Specification

This standard specifies performance requirements for LED lamps used for general lighting purposes.

Scope: This standard covers LED lamps including LED bulbs, LED tube lights, LED panels, and LED downlights intended for general lighting in residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

Clause 4: Classification
LED lamps shall be classified by:
- Wattage category: Low (1-10W), Medium (10-25W), High (25-100W)
- Colour temperature: Warm White (2700-3000K), Neutral White (4000-4500K), Cool White (6000-6500K)
- Colour Rendering Index (CRI): Minimum Ra ≥ 80 for indoor applications

Clause 5: Rated Values
Rated voltage: 240V AC (nominal), operating range 170-270V AC
Rated frequency: 50 Hz
Power factor: Minimum 0.9 for lamps above 5W
Total harmonic distortion (THD): Maximum 10% for lamps above 5W

Clause 6: Performance Requirements
Luminous efficacy: Minimum 100 lumens per watt for LED bulbs
Luminous flux maintenance: Minimum 70% of initial flux at 25,000 hours (L70B50)
Colour consistency: Within 7-step MacAdam ellipse
Start time: Maximum 1 second from switch-on
Colour rendering index: Ra ≥ 80 minimum

Clause 7: Safety Requirements
The lamp shall comply with IS 10322 or IS 15285 as applicable
Maximum surface temperature: 80°C during operation
Power supply: Built-in driver shall comply with IS 15885

Clause 8: Electromagnetic Compatibility
The lamp shall comply with IS 15935 (EMC requirements for lighting equipment)
Conducted emissions: Below limits specified in CISPR 15
Radiated emissions: Below limits specified in CISPR 15

Clause 9: Reliability
Minimum rated life: 25,000 hours
Number of switch cycles: Minimum 50,000 on-off cycles
Operating temperature range: -10°C to +50°C

Marking Requirements:
Each lamp shall be marked with:
- Rated voltage and frequency
- Rated power (W)
- Luminous flux (lumens)
- Colour temperature (K)
- CRI value
- Manufacturer name
- Country of origin
- Date of manufacture
- BIS standard number reference

Test Methods:
- Luminous flux measurement using integrating sphere
- Power measurement using digital power analyser
- Colour temperature measurement using spectroradiometer
- Life testing per IES LM-80 methodology
- Temperature rise measurement at rated voltage
