IS 16102:2022
Self-Ballasted Compact Fluorescent Lamps — Performance Requirements

Specification

This standard specifies performance requirements for self-ballasted compact fluorescent lamps (CFLs) for general lighting.

Scope: This standard applies to self-ballasted compact fluorescent lamps designed for operation on AC supply at 240V, 50 Hz, intended for general lighting.

Clause 4: Classification
CFLs shall be classified by:
- Wattage: 5W, 7W, 9W, 11W, 15W, 18W, 23W
- Base type: B22 (bayonet), E27 (Edison screw), GX24q (2-pin/4-pin)
- Colour temperature: Warm White (2700-3000K), Cool Daylight (6500K)
- Shape: Spiral, Tube, Candle, Globe

Clause 5: Rated Values
Rated voltage: 240V AC (nominal)
Operating range: 198-253V AC
Rated frequency: 50 Hz
Power factor: Minimum 0.5
CRI: Minimum 80

Clause 6: Performance Requirements
Luminous efficacy: Minimum 60 lumens per watt
Starting time: Maximum 5 seconds
Warm-up time to 60% flux: Maximum 120 seconds
Lumen maintenance at 5000 hours: Minimum 85% of initial flux
Rated average life: Minimum 6000 hours
Switching endurance: Minimum 10,000 cycles

Clauses 7-8: Electrical Requirements
Preheat starting: Recommended for long life
Power consumption at rated voltage: Maximum 115% of rated wattage
Total harmonic distortion: Maximum 30%
Flicker: Below 3% at 100 Hz

Clause 9: Safety Requirements
The lamp shall comply with IS 10322 (safety requirements for fluorescent lamps)
Maximum bulb surface temperature: 90°C
Ballast shall have thermal cut-off protection

Clause 10: Marking Requirements
Each lamp shall be marked with:
- Rated voltage and frequency (240V 50Hz)
- Rated power (W)
- Luminous flux (lumens)
- Colour temperature (K)
- CRI value
- Manufacturer name or trademark
- Country of origin
- Date code
- BIS mark reference

Test Methods:
- Luminous flux: Integrating sphere method (IS 11473)
- Power measurements: Digital power analyser
- Life testing: 3 hours on, 20 minutes off cycle
- Temperature: Thermocouple measurement
- Switching endurance: Automated on-off cycling
