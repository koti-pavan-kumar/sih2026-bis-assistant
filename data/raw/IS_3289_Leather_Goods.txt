IS 3289:2023
Finished Leather — Specification for Footwear and Leather Goods

Specification

This standard specifies requirements for finished leather used in footwear, bags, belts, and other leather goods.

Scope: This standard covers all types of finished leather including full grain, corrected grain, split, and bonded leather used for manufacturing footwear, handbags, belts, wallets, and other leather accessories.

Clause 4: Classification of Leather
Leather shall be classified as:
- Full Grain Leather: Complete grain surface retained
- Corrected Grain Leather: Surface corrected by buffing or embossing
- Top Grain Leather: Grain surface partially removed
- Split Leather: Lower layer of hide after splitting
- Bonded Leather: Reconstituted from leather fibres

Clause 5: Performance Requirements

Tensile Strength:
- Full grain: Minimum 15 N/mm²
- Corrected grain: Minimum 12 N/mm²
- Split leather: Minimum 10 N/mm²
- Bonded leather: Minimum 5 N/mm²

Elongation at Break:
- Minimum 15% for all types
- Maximum 80% for footwear leather

Tear Strength:
- Minimum 30 N (full grain)
- Minimum 20 N (corrected grain)
- Minimum 15 N (split)

Colour Fastness:
- To light (Xenon arc): Grade 4 minimum
- To rubbing (dry): Grade 4 minimum
- To rubbing (wet): Grade 3 minimum
- To water: Grade 3 minimum
- To perspiration: Grade 3 minimum

Flex Resistance:
- Flexometer test: Minimum 50,000 cycles without cracking
- Bally flex test: Minimum 20,000 cycles

Shrinkage Temperature:
- Vegetable tanned: Minimum 75°C
- Chrome tanned: Minimum 100°C

pH Value:
- 3.5 to 5.0 for chrome tanned
- 4.0 to 6.0 for vegetable tanned

Restricted Substances:
- Chromium (VI): Maximum 3 mg/kg
- Formaldehyde: Maximum 75 mg/kg
- Azo dyes releasing banned amines: Not detectable
- Total lead: Maximum 90 mg/kg

Clause 6: Dimensional Requirements
Leather thickness shall be specified by the buyer. Standard ranges:
- Footwear upper: 0.8 to 1.2 mm
- Sole leather: 2.0 to 4.0 mm
- Bag leather: 0.6 to 1.0 mm
- Belt leather: 2.5 to 4.5 mm

Marking Requirements:
- Grade mark (Full Grain / Corrected / Split)
- Manufacturer name or code
- Thickness in mm
- Tanning method (Chrome / Vegetable / Mixed)
- Date of manufacture

Test Methods:
- Tensile strength: IS 3378
- Tear strength: IS 3379
- Flex resistance: IS 3381
- Colour fastness: IS 3380
- Shrinkage temperature: IS 4018
- pH measurement: IS 4019
