Proposed Solution:
  • Complex BIS Standards — Standards are technical and hard to understand → AI-based Simplification explains technical requirements in simple language.
  • Scattered Information — BIS data spread across different sources → Unified Platform brings standards, certification & lab info together.
  • Changing Standards — Requirements can be revised or updated → Live Source Pipeline pulls info from official BIS sources automatically.
  • Finding Testing Labs — Users may struggle to find suitable BIS labs → Lab Directory with location-based finder & Google Maps directions.
  • Complex Certification Process — Users may not know the steps required → Step-by-Step Guidance for ISI Mark, Hallmark & Eco Mark.
  • AI Hallucinations — AI may generate incorrect regulatory information → Source-Grounded RAG bases responses on verified BIS documents.
  • Language Barrier — BIS info mostly in English only → 22 Indian Languages with real-time auto-detect & translation pipeline.
  • Different User Needs — MSMEs, manufacturers & individuals differ → Context-Aware Assistance tailors guidance per user product.

Innovation and Uniqueness:
  • Source-Grounded RAG Architecture — ONNX embeddings + FAISS vector store ensures responses are hallucination-free and cite official BIS document sections.
  • Live BIS Information Pipeline — Auto-fetches and indexes new standards from bis.gov.in, keeping the knowledge base always up-to-date.
  • 22 Indian Languages — Real-time auto-detect → translate → RAG → translate-back pipeline. Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali and more.
  • 6-Section Structured Responses — Every answer includes standards, testing, locations, legal basis, QC process, and documents — not just plain text.
  • Unified Compliance Ecosystem — Brings standards, certification guidance, laboratory finder, and AI assistance into a single platform.
